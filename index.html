❓ Help

Do I register inside this app?

Almost — this app is for browsing and choosing. When you tap Register, the official WCCCD registration form opens with your class already selected. You fill in your personal information there and submit. Each class is one quick form submission.

What do classes cost?

Class fees are confirmed during registration. Contact the Continuing Education office with any questions about fees.

Do I have to retype my information for every class?

No — on the review page, tap Fill in your info once. Your details are saved on this device and pre-filled on every class's registration form. On a shared or public computer, tap Clear my info when you're done.

Where did my list go?

Your class list is saved automatically on the device and browser you're using — it will be here when you come back. It won't follow you to a different phone or computer.

A class says it can't be selected on the form

A small number of classes aren't in the registration form's course list yet. For those, contact the Continuing Education office to register.
